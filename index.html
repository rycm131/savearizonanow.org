Serving Our Community

Free essentials for Arizona neighbors going through a hard time.

Save Arizona Now gives away food, diapers, baby wipes and household items at free community giveaways across the Phoenix area. Everything is free, and everyone who helps is a volunteer.

What we give

The basics a household runs out of first.

When money is tight, these are the things families go without. We hand them out at no cost.

Food

Groceries and pantry items to help stretch a week's meals.

Diapers

Diapers for babies and toddlers, one of the biggest costs for young families.

Baby wipes

The everyday item that food assistance programs do not cover.

Household items

Donated goods for the home, passed along to someone who can use them.

How it works

From a donated pallet to a neighbor's car.

  1. We collect

    Companies give us overstock they cannot sell, and people in the community donate items they no longer need.

  2. We store and sort

    Volunteers pick up the donations, sort them and keep them ready for the next giveaway.

  3. We give it away

    Each month we set up a free giveaway in a Phoenix-area city, hosted with a local business or church.

Giveaway dates and locations are posted on this page as they are scheduled.

Get involved

Three ways to help.

We are new in Arizona and growing one giveaway at a time. Every pallet, parking lot and pair of hands matters.

Donate goods or funds

Businesses: send us your overstock, returns or short-dated product. Individuals: give household items in good condition, or a financial gift that buys diapers and food.

Host a giveaway

Churches and local businesses can lend a parking lot or hall for a morning. We bring the goods, the tables and the volunteers.

Volunteer

Help pick up donations, sort them, or hand them out on giveaway day. A couple of hours makes a real difference.

To do any of these, get in touch and tell us how you would like to help.

About us

Neighbors helping neighbors, with no one on the payroll.

Save Arizona Now is an Arizona nonprofit that gives essentials to people in financial hardship. We held our first Arizona giveaway in the fall of 2026.

  • A 501(c)(3) public charity. Donations are tax-deductible to the extent the law allows.
  • Run entirely by volunteers. No one is paid.
  • Everything we receive is given away free.
  • Based in the West Valley and serving cities across the Phoenix area.
